About IC

We work for you since 2023 Industrial around the world

Welcome to the essence behind our identity. Let’s unfold the inspiration and meaning behind every element of who we are.

Established in year 2023 in GIDC, industrial area Panoli, Interpose Chem is involved in the manufacturing and development of wide range of high- quality intermediates in its ultramodern state of art facilities.

Our mission is to develop and deliver high-quality pharmaceutical intermediates through innovation, precision, and integrity—empowering the creation of safe and effective medicines for global health.

To be the vital link between molecules and human health, driving innovation in pharmaceutical intermediates that enable life-saving medicines worldwide.
